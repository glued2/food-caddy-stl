1. food_caddy_k1se_body_216mm.stl
2. food_caddy_k1se_flip_lid_216mm.stl

Design update after the first test print:
- The bag-retainer frame in food_caddy_k1se_liner_hanger_plate.stl now has a
  uniform 2.8 mm thickness with flush grip tabs, instead of a thinner ring with
  a raised tab that could separate when removed from the bed.
- The hinge-retainer collar is now fully round and prints standing on its flat
  circular face, so the pin bore prints vertically instead of needing a small
  side lug that printed poorly on its side.
- The lid's rear hinge spine and front seating tab now have wider, full-
  thickness base pads bonded to the lid skin, to avoid the same kind of
  shear-off failure the retainer frame tab had.
- The lid's rear hinge knuckle had a separate bug where its round tube dipped
  0.3 mm below the bed plane in the model coordinates. This forced the whole
  lid to be lifted 0.3 mm off the print bed to compensate, which is very
  likely why the first couple of layers printed badly on the test print. This
  is now fixed: the knuckle no longer dips below the lid skin, so the lid
  sits flush on the bed.
- Alternative accessory plates are available if you don't want to reprint
  parts that already succeeded:
  - food_caddy_k1se_accessories_everything.stl: bag-retainer frame, hanger
    ears, collar, hinge rail, and hinge pin all on one plate.
  - food_caddy_k1se_accessories_no_rails.stl: the same, minus the hinge rail,
    since the rail already printed successfully on the first attempt.
  - food_caddy_k1se_failed_parts_only.stl: just the bag-retainer frame and
    collar, i.e. only the two parts that actually failed the first time.

Current design intent:
- Print the main bin upright, open side up.
- Do not print the main bin sideways. Sideways printing would need extensive
  support inside the bin, would make the hinge holes rougher/less accurate, and
  would put the hanging rim and bottom joint in a less useful layer orientation.
- The main bin no longer has integrated hinge barrels or vertical ribs. That
  keeps the largest print simple, upright, and support-free.
- The top lip is a shallow 8 mm tapered flare rather than a sudden horizontal
  shelf. This avoids the rounded-corner gaps/overhangs from the earlier lip and
  keeps the rim printable upright without support.
- Strength is now coming from geometry rather than bulk: thin walls, a small
  closed rim/bead, a slightly thicker floor, and separate load-bearing hanger
  and hinge parts.
- The lid is intentionally lightweight because it only acts as an odour cover.
  It uses a thin 0.8 mm skin with shallow stiffening beads rather than a thick
  solid plate.
- The body now has a shallow inner seating ledge at the top rim. The lid is
  slightly smaller than the outer rim so it sits on that ledge when closed,
  rather than relying on the hinge position alone to hold it flush.
- The rear hinge rail is a separate clip-on part. It is a U-channel that wraps
  over the rear top rim of the bin, so the main bin itself does not need hinge
  barrels or support material. It is included in the hinge kit plate.
- The previous integrated ribs were removed because they did not improve the
  useful layer direction when printed as part of the upright bin.

The package only includes K1 SE-relevant STLs. The oversized 230 mm body and
226 mm lid have been removed because they do not fit the usual K1 SE 220 x 220
mm build plate.

The K1SE body is intentionally 216 mm wide so it prints on the K1 SE with slicer
clearance. It will not
hang across the 230 mm support gap by itself. Use the two printed hanger ears
from the liner/hanger plate. Each ear is a C-channel clip: the open slot
slides sideways over the 4 mm top flange, one from the left side and one from the
right side. The thicker outside edge faces away from the caddy and becomes the
new hanging ledge. The ear slot includes a tapered guide face to match the
shallow flared rim. The small lower hook sits under the flange so the ear cannot
just lift off when you remove the liner.

Once both ears are fitted, they extend the assembled hanging width back to about
230 mm after printing, while keeping every individual print within the K1 SE
build area. If the ears feel loose, add a small drop of CA glue after test-fitting
the caddy in the kitchen bin.

Separate K1 SE spare-part files are included for the bag retainer, hinge pin,
and hinge collar in case a small part breaks or the fit needs experimenting.

Hinge:
The separate rear hinge rail has two outside hinge knuckles. The lid has one
centre hinge knuckle. First slide/press the rear hinge rail over the back rim of
the bin: the U-channel wraps over the rim, with one wall inside the caddy and one
wall outside the caddy. The small internal barbs are there to grip under the rim;
if the fit is too tight, lightly sand the barbs.

The rail hinge holes are circular. Once the hinge rail is seated on the bin, line up the lid knuckle between the two
rail knuckles, then slide the hinge pin through all three from one side. The
square head on one end stops the pin moving in one direction. Push the small
retaining collar onto the other end so the pin cannot walk out during use.

The hinge holes are 3.7 mm diameter. The printed hinge pin is 2.9 mm diameter,
leaving clearance for typical FDM print tolerances. The retaining collar is a
friction fit on the pin. If it is too tight, lightly sand the pin end; if it is too
loose, add a tiny drop of CA glue, hot glue, or a softened filament mushroom on
the very end after confirming the lid swings freely.

Printing:
- Print the body upright, open side up.
- Print the rear hinge rail separately.
- Print the lid flat, top face down or up depending on the finish you prefer.
- Print the hinge pin lying flat.
- Print the bag-retainer ring flat.
- PETG is preferred for kitchen use; PLA is fine for a fit test.

Bag retainer:
Put the liner into the caddy, fold the top of the liner over the rim, then press
the bag-retainer ring into the opening to trap the liner neatly below the lid.
The ring is sized to sit inside the newer flared rim and below the lid seating
ledge, so it should not hold the lid open. Its outer footprint is about
204 x 164 mm including the small grip tabs.
